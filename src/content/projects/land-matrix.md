Worked with the Land Matrix consortium on a product-strategy reset and a refreshed data architecture for their global deals database. The aim was to make the observatory legible to policy audiences without losing the rigour researchers rely on.

The engagement paired a user-research phase with a rebuild of the underlying schema and query layer, and ended with a roadmap the consortium carried forward independently.
